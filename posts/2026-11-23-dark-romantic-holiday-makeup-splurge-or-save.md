After a few years of clean girl skin and barely there gloss, makeup has taken a moodier turn. Pinterest calls it Vamp Romantic, beauty editors have been calling it soft goth or gothic romance, and searches for dark romantic makeup have jumped by around 160 percent. The look is simple to describe: a wine or berry lip with a glossy finish, kohl smudged softly around the eyes, and skin that still looks fresh and flushed underneath.

What keeps it romantic rather than costume is softness. No sharp wings, no perfect outlines, no flat matte black. Edges are blurred, shine is welcome, and the colors are the ones you see in a glass of red wine or a velvet ribbon.

## Step 1: Smudged kohl

The eyes in this look are lived in, not precise. A soft kohl pencil, pressed into the lash line and blurred out, gives that just-after-midnight shadow in about a minute.

How to apply it: hold the pencil flat and wiggle it along the root of your upper lashes. Smudge it upward with a fingertip, a small brush or the pencil's own smudger before it sets. Add a little to the outer third of the lower lash line and soften it too. The goal is a haze, not a line.

## Step 2: Smoky plum eyes

A dark romantic eye does not need a full smoky eye. A wash of plum, wine or bronze over the lid, blended into the kohl, adds depth and makes the eyes look softly shadowed rather than heavily made up.

How to apply it: sweep a mid-tone shade over the lid with a fluffy brush. Press a deeper shade into the outer corner and along the lash line on top of your kohl, then blend the edges until there is no visible line. Finish with a tap of shimmer in the center of the lid to catch candlelight.

## Step 3: Line the lip in berry

The lip liner is the secret to a dark lip that lasts through dinner. Filling the whole lip with liner first gives the lipstick something to grip, and leaves a stain behind when the shine wears off. It also keeps deep colors from bleeding past the lip line.

How to apply it: outline your lips with light, feathery strokes, then fill them in completely. For a softer, more romantic edge, press your fingertip around the outline to blur it slightly.

Gloss is what turns a dark lip from dramatic to romantic. The shine catches the light and softens the color, giving that juicy, just-sipped-wine effect Pinterest has been full of.

How to apply it: add gloss to the center of your top and bottom lips only, then press them together. Keeping it off the edges stops the color from sliding.

## Keep the rest soft

The dark romantic look depends on balance. Keep your skin fresh and luminous rather than heavily matte, add a flush of berry or rose blush high on the cheeks, and skip harsh contour. A coat of mascara is enough for lashes. The softness of the skin is what keeps the dark eyes and lips feeling romantic.

## FAQ

### What is dark romantic makeup?

It is a moody, soft look trending on Pinterest as Vamp Romantic. It pairs a wine, plum or berry lip with a glossy finish, softly smudged kohl around the eyes, and fresh, flushed skin. The edges are blurred on purpose, so it reads romantic rather than gothic.

### How do I keep a dark lipstick from bleeding?

Line and fully fill your lips with a matching liner first, apply the lipstick in thin layers and blot between them. Keep gloss to the center of the lips, away from the edges.

### Can I wear dark lipstick if I have never tried it?

Yes. Start by dabbing the color on with your fingertip for a softer, stained look, then build up once you are comfortable. A deep berry gloss on its own is another easy first step.

### Should I wear a dark lip and a smoky eye together?

For the vamp romantic look, a softly smudged eye and a dark lip go together, as long as the eye stays blurry and not too heavy. If you want just one focal point, keep the kohl very light and let the lip do the talking.

### Is the dark romantic look only for evening?

It works best for holiday parties and dinners, but a daytime version is easy: a berry gloss on its own, a little brown kohl smudged into the lash line, and fresh skin.
