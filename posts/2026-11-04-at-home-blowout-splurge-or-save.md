A salon blowout is lovely and also gone in three days. An at-home routine pays for itself, as long as you buy for the way you really do your hair, not the way you plan to.

Before you shop, ask one question: how many days a week will you use this? A tool you use twice a week can justify a higher price. One that comes out for birthdays cannot.

## The tool

This is the biggest decision and the biggest gap in price.

**The splurge:** [Dyson Airwrap](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Dyson+Airwrap) is the tool that made multi-styling a category. It is an investment, so check the listing is from Dyson's own Amazon store before buying.

**The everyday:** [Revlon One-Step Volumizer](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Revlon+One-Step+Volumizer) dries and styles in one pass for a fraction of the price.

## The heat protection

Do not skip this step, whichever tool you own. Spray it through damp hair before any heat.

**The splurge:** [Kérastase Thermique heat protectant](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Kerastase+Thermique+Heat+Protectant) brings the salon feel home, down to the scent.

**The everyday:** [TRESemmé Thermal Creations Heat Tamer Spray](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=TRESemme+Thermal+Creations+Heat+Tamer+Spray) is a simple drugstore staple that is easy to restock.

## The overnight care

The way you sleep decides whether your blowout lasts until day three. A smooth pillowcase creates less friction than cotton.

**The splurge:** [Slip silk pillowcase](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Slip+Silk+Pillowcase) is the luxury version, and a lovely gift for anyone who has everything.

**The everyday:** [Kitsch satin pillowcase](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Kitsch+Satin+Pillowcase) offers the same smooth feel in an easy-care fabric.

## The verdict

Spend on the tool only if you will truly use it each week. Otherwise, the everyday brush and a good pillowcase will take you a long way.
