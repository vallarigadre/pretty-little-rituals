## FAQ

### What's the best lip product to give as a gift?

An overnight lip mask is the safest choice, because almost everyone has dry lips in winter and it doesn't depend on shade. The LANEIGE Lip Sleeping Mask is the classic pick, and Burt's Bees is a lovely everyday option.

### Is a lip oil or a lip gloss better as a gift?

Lip oils usually feel lighter and more comfortable, while glosses tend to give more shine and color. If you aren't sure what she likes, a tinted lip oil is a gentle middle ground.

### How do I choose a shade for someone else?

Stick to sheer, flexible shades. Clear, soft pink and sheer berry tints like Black Honey suit a wide range of skin tones and are much easier to get right than a bold lipstick.

### Are lip products good stocking stuffers?

Yes. They're small, useful and easy to fit in a stocking. Squeeze balms, a single lip oil or a small overnight treatment all work beautifully.
