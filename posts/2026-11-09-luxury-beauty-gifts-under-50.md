## How to make a small gift look expensive

- **Check the seller.** For luxury brands, look for "Ships from and sold by Amazon" or the brand's official store on the listing, so you know the gift is authentic.
- **Pair a splurge with an everyday.** A Laneige lip mask with an e.l.f. lip oil, or L'Occitane hand cream with a Kitsch scrunchie, makes a bundle that looks thoughtfully curated.
- **Choose a tight color story.** Wrap in black, blush and warm white with a silk or velvet ribbon. Two or three colors look far more luxurious than a busy print.
- **Add a note.** One line on why you chose it ("for your nightstand", "for your handbag") makes even a mini feel personal.

## FAQ

### What beauty gifts look expensive but aren't?

Minis, travel sizes and gift trios from prestige brands. They give her the luxury name and packaging in a smaller format, which is why they feel more lavish than their size suggests.

### How do I know luxury beauty on Amazon is authentic?

Check the listing for "Ships from and sold by Amazon" or the brand's official store. Read a few recent reviews, and be cautious of third-party sellers with very few ratings.

### What is a safe beauty gift if I don't know her skin type?

Hand cream, lip masks and silk hair ties are the safest picks, because almost everyone uses them. Cleansing balms are a good choice too, but avoid strong actives if you don't know her routine.
