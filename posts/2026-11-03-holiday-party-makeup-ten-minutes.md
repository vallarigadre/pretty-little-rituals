Party season has a way of arriving at 6:40 pm, twenty minutes before you need to leave. This look uses four products and takes about ten minutes. It relies on glow and a good lip rather than any real technique.

## Glow first

A luminous base does the work of a full face. Dab it on the high points of your face, or wear it all over for a sheer finish.

**The splurge:** [Charlotte Tilbury Hollywood Flawless Filter](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Charlotte+Tilbury+Hollywood+Flawless+Filter) is the one you have seen everywhere, and it arrived in Amazon's Premium Beauty store in 2025.

**The everyday:** [L'Oréal Paris Lumi Glotion](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=L%27Oreal+Paris+Lumi+Glotion) gives a similar lit-from-within finish at a drugstore price.

## Then cheeks

Holiday light is flattering to a warmer flush. Tap a cream or liquid blush onto the apples of your cheeks and blend upward with your fingers.

**The splurge:** [Charlotte Tilbury Matte Beauty Blush Wand](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Charlotte+Tilbury+Beauty+Blush+Wand) is easy to use and a small piece of everyday luxury in your bag.

**The everyday:** [e.l.f. Halo Glow Liquid Blush](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=e.l.f.+Halo+Glow+Liquid+Blush) offers a wand applicator and a soft finish for far less.

## One coat of lashes

Here is the honest one: mascara dries out in a few months, so an expensive tube rarely earns its keep. This is the place to save.

**The splurge:** [Lancôme Hypnôse mascara](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Lancome+Hypnose+Mascara) is a long-standing department store favorite if you like a little luxury at the mirror.

**The everyday:** [Maybelline Lash Sensational Sky High](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Maybelline+Lash+Sensational+Sky+High+Mascara) is a well-loved drugstore tube with a flexible brush.

## Finish with the lip

The lip is what people see across the table, and a beautiful case is half the pleasure. This is where a luxury buy feels most worth it.

**The splurge:** [Charlotte Tilbury Matte Revolution lipstick in Pillow Talk](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Charlotte+Tilbury+Matte+Revolution+Pillow+Talk) is a famous nude-pink in a rose gold case.

**The everyday:** [NYX Butter Gloss](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=NYX+Butter+Gloss) has a soft shine and a shade range that makes it easy to buy two.

## The verdict

Spend on the lipstick, because you will reapply it in public. Save on mascara, because you replace it often anyway. The rest is up to you.
