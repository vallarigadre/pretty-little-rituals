## What people love, and what they don't

Before the picks, a quick look at what real shoppers say about Aesop across retailers and review sites.

The love is mostly about **scent and feel**. Reviewers describe the fragrances as earthy, herbal and calm, and the hand washes as cleansing without that tight, stripped feeling. The packaging is a big part of the appeal: it looks beautiful on a counter and feels special as a gift.

The complaints are consistent too. **Price** comes up the most, and many people buy Aesop as a treat or a present rather than a weekly staple. Some find the hand balm too light for very dry, cracked hands, some mention pumps that leak, and a few people with sensitive skin found the scents too strong for them.

That pattern shapes the picks below. Aesop shines where scent and ritual matter. It's harder to justify where you simply need something to work hard and last.

## Worth the splurge?

Yes, in the right places. Aesop is worth it where you'll notice it every day: the hand wash by a sink you look at, or a gift for someone who loves a beautiful home. The scent and the bottle are what you're paying for, and that's what reviewers love most.
