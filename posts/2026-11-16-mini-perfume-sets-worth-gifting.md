## How to choose the right set

- **Think about what she already wears.** If her signature is warm and sweet, stay in the cozy section. If she rarely wears perfume, start with mists.
- **Count the bottles.** More minis means more variety. Fewer, bigger minis mean she can really live with each scent.
- **Check the format.** Some minis are sprays and some are splashes or rollerballs. Sprays are the easiest to use on the go.
- **Add a note.** A card that says "find your favorite, and I'll get you the full bottle" makes a mini set feel extra thoughtful.

## FAQ

### Are mini perfume sets a good gift?

Yes, especially when you don't know her exact taste. A set lets her try several scents, find a favorite and carry a small bottle in her bag. Many shoppers see minis as a low-risk way to give a luxury name.

### What is the difference between a mini and a perfume sample?

Samples are usually tiny vials meant for a few wears. Minis are small versions of the real bottle, often 5 to 10 ml, with enough perfume for many wears, and they usually look like the full size.

### Are perfume mists the same as perfume?

Not quite. Mists usually have a lighter concentration, so they feel softer and many people reapply them through the day. They are great for layering under a perfume or for anyone who prefers a gentle scent.

### Which mini perfume set is best for someone who loves vanilla?
