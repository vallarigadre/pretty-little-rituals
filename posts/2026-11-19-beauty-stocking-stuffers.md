## FAQ

### What are good beauty stocking stuffers for women?

Lip balms and lip masks, pimple patches, under eye patches, mini mascaras and travel-size dry shampoo are all reliable picks. They are small, useful and easy to use up, so nothing goes to waste.

### Are pimple patches a good gift?

They make a surprisingly popular stocking stuffer, because almost everyone uses them and they are rarely something people splurge on for themselves. Choose a cute or sleek pack so it feels like a gift, not a pharmacy run.

### How do I know luxury beauty on Amazon is authentic?

Check that the listing says "Ships from and sold by Amazon" or that it comes from the brand's official store. Recent reviews are worth a quick scan too.

### What if I don't know her skin type?

Stick with lip care, eye patches and dry shampoo, which suit most people. Save targeted skincare for someone whose routine you already know.
