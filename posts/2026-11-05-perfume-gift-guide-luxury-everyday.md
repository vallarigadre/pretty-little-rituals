Perfume is one of the most personal gifts you can give, which makes it both lovely and a little risky. The trick is to buy for a mood rather than a person. Think about what she already wears: is it fresh, sweet or warm?

Here are three moods, each with a luxury bottle and an easier everyday option.

## If she loves fresh and clean

**The splurge:** [Dolce & Gabbana Light Blue Eau de Toilette](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Dolce+Gabbana+Light+Blue+Eau+de+Toilette) is a citrus-and-apple classic, and it has been a safe, beautiful gift for years.

**The everyday:** [Clean Reserve body mist](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Clean+Reserve+Fragrance+Mist) keeps the airy feeling in a lighter form she can spritz without thinking.

## If she loves sweet and creamy

**The splurge:** [Lancôme La Vie Est Belle Eau de Parfum](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Lancome+La+Vie+Est+Belle+Eau+de+Parfum) is a sweet, iris-led scent.

**The everyday:** [Sol de Janeiro Brazilian Crush Cheirosa 62 mist](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Sol+de+Janeiro+Cheirosa+62+Perfume+Mist) has that warm, gourmand scent people always ask about.

## If she loves warm and bold

**The splurge:** [Yves Saint Laurent Black Opium Eau de Parfum](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Yves+Saint+Laurent+Black+Opium+Eau+de+Parfum) is coffee, vanilla and evening plans in one glossy black bottle.

**The everyday:** [Lattafa Khamrah Eau de Parfum](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Lattafa+Khamrah+Eau+de+Parfum) has become a favorite with fragrance lovers for its rich, spiced warmth at a much lower price.

## Before you buy

Luxury fragrance is a category where the seller really matters. Check that the listing is sold by the brand or shipped by Amazon, read a few recent reviews, and give yourself time before the holidays in case you need a return.

If you are unsure of her taste, a travel size or a discovery set is the safest luxury of all.
