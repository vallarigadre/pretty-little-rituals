Pretty Little Rituals is for women who love beauty but do not believe every step of a routine needs the most expensive option. Every story pairs a luxury pick with an everyday one, across skincare, K-beauty, makeup, hair, fragrance and gifts, so you can choose where your money goes.

## Who picks the products

I'm V, Vallari Gadre. Before this blog I spent ten years in fashion retail buying and merchandise planning, deciding which products earned a place on the shelf and which were left behind. I bring that same eye to beauty: reading ingredient lists and real listings, weighing thousands of shopper reviews, and asking one question of every luxury product. Is the extra money buying something you will notice?

## What we promise

- **Two price points, every time.** A splurge and an everyday pick, with a plain explanation of what separates them.
- **Real products, direct links.** Every pick links to its own Amazon listing. For luxury products, check the listing says it ships from and is sold by Amazon or the brand store.
- **A short list.** A few well-chosen finds per story, not fifty.
- **Honest about how picks are made.** Products are chosen from their ingredient lists, brand reputation and customer reviews. If we have not tried something ourselves, we will not pretend otherwise.
- **Beauty, not medicine.** Nothing here is medical advice. For a skin concern, please see a dermatologist, and patch test anything new.
- **Open about affiliate links.** Some links earn a small commission. It never changes the price you pay, and it is always disclosed.
