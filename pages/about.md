Pretty Little Rituals is for women who love beauty but do not believe every step of a routine needs the most expensive option. Every story pairs a luxury pick with an everyday one, across skincare, makeup, hair and fragrance, so you can choose where your money goes.

It is written by Vallari, who spent a decade in retail buying and planning, deciding which products deserve shelf space and which do not. That habit carries straight into every list on this site.

## What we promise

- **Two price points, every time.** A splurge and an everyday pick, with a plain explanation of what separates them.
- **A short list.** A few well-chosen finds per story, not fifty.
- **Honest about how picks are made.** Products are chosen from their ingredient lists, brand reputation and customer reviews. If we have not tried something ourselves, we will not pretend otherwise.
- **Beauty, not medicine.** Nothing here is medical advice. For a skin concern, please see a dermatologist, and patch test anything new.
- **Open about affiliate links.** Some links earn a small commission. It never changes the price you pay, and it is always disclosed.
